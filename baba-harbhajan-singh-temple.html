About the place

The Baba Harbhajan Singh Temple is a memorial shrine in the high mountain area between Nathu La and Jelepla. It is an important place for many visitors and remembers Indian Army soldier Harbhajan Singh.

Things to remember: This is a high-altitude and restricted-area region. Visitors may need a permit and must follow current Army and local instructions. Check access rules before planning a trip.
