Why You Should Visit Tashi Viewpoint

Located about 8 km from Gangtok, Sikkim at an elevation of 6,500 feet, Tashi Viewpoint was established by former Chogyal Tashi Namgyal. Surrounded by lush forests and hills along the North Sikkim Highway, it offers a peaceful escape with panoramic views of the Himalayas.

Its biggest highlight is the breathtaking view of Mount Kanchenjunga—the world's third-highest peak—which glows in shades of gold, orange, and pink during early morning sunrise.

Highlights & Visitor Tips

  • Best Time to Visit: Arrive early at sunrise on clear days for the sharpest views of Kanchenjunga; spring brings blooming Rhododendrons.
  • Nearby Attractions: Conveniently situated on the sightseeing route toward Phodong Monastery and North Sikkim, with local tea and snack stalls nearby.
  • Safety & Etiquette: Carry warm clothing, stay away from steep edges, and keep the area clean and litter-free.
