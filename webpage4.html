Saramsa Garden

Located about 14 km south of Gangtok near Ranipool in the Pakyong District, Saramsa Garden (historically known as the Ipecac Garden) is a scenic terraced botanical sanctuary established in 1922 by the Sikkim Forest Department under Rai Saheb Bhim Bahadur Pradhan.

Originally a fruit orchard for the Chogyal and later a medicinal ipecac plantation from 1940 into the 1970s, the garden evolved into a major orchidarium and floriculture center—especially after hosting the 2008 International Floriculture Show.

Highlights & Visitor Tips

  • Orchidarium & Flora: Greenhouses protect Eastern Himalayan orchids like Cymbidium, Dendrobium, and Vanda, alongside seasonal flowers, fernries, and conifers.
  • Best Time to Visit: Spring and autumn bring vibrant seasonal blooms and pleasant terraced walks.
  • Visitor Care: Stick to garden paths, avoid picking plants, and dispose of litter responsibly.
