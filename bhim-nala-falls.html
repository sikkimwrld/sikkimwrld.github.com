About the place

Bhim Nala Falls, also known as Amitabh Bachchan Falls, is a tall waterfall near Lachung in North Sikkim. Water flows down the mountain rocks, making it a popular stop for visitors travelling in the area.

Things to remember: The road can be affected by weather. Check local travel advice before setting out, keep the area clean, and follow any safety instructions.
