About the place

Saramsa Garden, also called Ipecac Garden, is a garden near Gangtok, in the Ranipool area. It is known for its plants, flowers and quiet green spaces. It is a nice place for a relaxing walk and family photographs.

Things to remember: Please follow garden rules, do not pick flowers, and check opening times before visiting.
