About the place

Tashi Viewpoint is a popular place to see the mountains from above Gangtok. On a clear day, visitors may see the Kanchenjunga range and surrounding valleys. There are also small shops nearby.

Things to remember: Mountain views depend on the weather. Go early for a better chance of clear skies and take care near the viewpoint edges.
